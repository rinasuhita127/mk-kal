# Soal tentang determinan matriks, dekomposisi matriks, dan invers matriks 3×3

## A. Soal Determinan Matriks 3×3

### Soal 1
Hitung determinan dari matriks berikut:

$$
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
1 & 2 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Jawaban

Menghitung determinan menggunakan ekspansi baris 1

$$
\det(A)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}(-1)^{1+k}a_{1k}M_{1k}
$$

$$
=(-1)^{1+1}a_{11}M_{11}
+(-1)^{1+2}a_{12}M_{12}
+(-1)^{1+3}a_{13}M_{13}
$$

$$
=2(4\cdot1-5\cdot2)-1(0\cdot1-5\cdot1)+3(0\cdot2-4\cdot1)
$$

$$
=2(-6)-(-5)+3(-4)
$$

$$
=-12+5-12
$$

$$
=-19
$$

$$
\det(A)=-19
$$

### Soal 2
Tentukan nilai determinan matriks:

$$
B =
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & 2 & 1 \\
1 & 0 & 4 \\
2 & 5 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Jawaban

$$
\det(B)
=(-1)^{1+1}a_{11}M_{11}
+(-1)^{1+2}a_{12}M_{12}
+(-1)^{1+3}a_{13}M_{13}
$$

$$
=3(0\cdot1-4\cdot5)-2(1\cdot1-4\cdot2)+1(1\cdot5-0\cdot2)
$$

$$
=3(-20)-2(-7)+1(5)
$$

$$
=-60+14+5
$$

$$
=-41
$$

$$
\det(B)=-41
$$

## B. Soal Dekomposisi Matriks (LU Decomposition)

### Soal 4

Lakukan dekomposisi LU terhadap matriks:

$$
A=
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 4 & 2\\
1 & 5 & 2\\
1 & 2 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

dengan bentuk

$$
A=LU
$$

### Jawaban

Misalkan

$$
L=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0\\
l_{21} & 1 & 0\\
l_{31} & l_{32} & 1
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
U=
\begin{bmatrix}
u_{11} & u_{12} & u_{13}\\
0 & u_{22} & u_{23}\\
0 & 0 & u_{33}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Langkah 1**

Dari baris pertama,

$$
u_{11}=2,\qquad
u_{12}=4,\qquad
u_{13}=2
$$

**Langkah 2**

Hitung elemen kolom pertama matriks \(L\)

$$
l_{21}=\frac{1}{2},\qquad
l_{31}=\frac{1}{2}
$$

**Langkah 3**

Hitung elemen baris kedua matriks \(U\)

$$
u_{22}=5-\frac12(4)=3
$$

$$
u_{23}=2-\frac12(2)=1
$$

**Langkah 4**

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks \(L\)

$$
l_{32}=\frac{2-\frac12(4)}{3}=0
$$

**Langkah 5**

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks \(U\)

$$
u_{33}=4-\frac12(2)-0(1)=3
$$

Sehingga diperoleh

$$
L=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0&0\\
\frac12&1&0\\
\frac12&0&1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

dan

$$
U=
\begin{bmatrix}
2&4&2\\
0&3&1\\
0&0&3
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Jadi,

$$
A=LU.
$$

### Soal 5

Tentukan matriks \(L\) dan \(U\) dari

$$
B=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&2&1\\
2&5&3\\
4&10&8
\end{bmatrix}
$$

menggunakan metode eliminasi Gauss.

### Jawaban

Matriks awal

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1&2&1\\
2&5&3\\
4&10&8
\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Eliminasi pertama**

$$
R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1
$$

$$
R_3\leftarrow R_3-4R_1
$$

diperoleh

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1&2&1\\
0&1&1\\
0&2&4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Faktor eliminasi:

$$
l_{21}=2,\qquad
l_{31}=4
$$

**Eliminasi kedua**

$$
R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_2
$$

diperoleh

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1&2&1\\
0&1&1\\
0&0&2
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Faktor eliminasi

$$
l_{32}=2
$$

Sehingga

$$
U=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&2&1\\
0&1&1\\
0&0&2
\end{bmatrix}
$$

dan

$$
L=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0&0\\
2&1&0\\
4&2&1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Jadi,

$$
B=LU.
$$

\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
U=

\end{bmatrix}

**Langkah 1**

Baris pertama

$$
u_{11}=4,\qquad
u_{12}=2,\qquad
u_{13}=0
$$

**Langkah 2**

Hitung elemen kolom pertama matriks \(L\)

$$
l_{21}=\frac24=\frac12,\qquad
l_{31}=0
$$

**Langkah 3**

Hitung elemen baris kedua matriks \(U\)

$$
u_{22}=5-\frac12(2)=4
$$

$$
u_{23}=1-\frac12(0)=1
$$

**Langkah 4**

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks \(L\)

$$
l_{32}=\frac{1-0(2)}{4}=\frac14
$$

**Langkah 5**

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks \(U\)

u_{33}=3-0(0)-\frac14(1)=\frac{11}{4}

## C. Soal Invers Matriks 3×3
### Soal 7

Tentukan invers dari matriks berikut.

$$
A=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 1\\
0 & 1 & 1\\
2 & 3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Menggunakan metode adjoin atau eliminasi Gauss-Jordan.

### Jawaban
Menghitung menggunakan adjoin.

Rumus matriks adjoin:

$$
(\operatorname{Adj}A)_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}M_{ji}
$$

Sehingga,

$$
\operatorname{Adj}(A)=
\begin{bmatrix}
(-1)^{1+1}M_{11} & (-1)^{2+1}M_{21} & (-1)^{3+1}M_{31}\\
(-1)^{1+2}M_{12} & (-1)^{2+2}M_{22} & (-1)^{3+2}M_{32}\\
(-1)^{1+3}M_{13} & (-1)^{2+3}M_{23} & (-1)^{3+3}M_{33}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
M_{11} & -M_{21} & M_{31}\\
-M_{12} & M_{22} & -M_{32}\\
M_{13} & -M_{23} & M_{33}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\operatorname{Adj}(A)=
\begin{bmatrix}
(1\cdot4-1\cdot3) & -(2\cdot4-1\cdot3) & (2\cdot1-1\cdot1)\\
-(0\cdot4-1\cdot2) & (1\cdot4-1\cdot2) & -(1\cdot1-1\cdot0)\\
(0\cdot3-1\cdot2) & -(1\cdot3-2\cdot2) & (1\cdot1-2\cdot0)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
(4-3) & -(8-3) & (2-1)\\
-(0-2) & (4-2) & -(1-0)\\
(0-2) & -(3-4) & (1-0)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -5 & 1\\
2 & 2 & -1\\
-2 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Hitung determinan:

$$
\det(A)
=1(1\cdot4-1\cdot3)-2(0\cdot4-1\cdot2)+1(0\cdot3-1\cdot2)
$$

$$
=1(4-3)-2(0-2)+1(0-2)
$$

$$
=1+4-2
$$

$$
=3
$$

Menentukan invers:

Rumus matriks invers:

$$
A^{-1}
=
\frac{1}{\det(A)}
\operatorname{Adj}(A)
$$

$$
A^{-1}
=
\frac13
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -5 & 1\\
2 & 2 & -1\\
-2 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac13 & -\frac53 & \frac13\\
\frac23 & \frac23 & -\frac13\\
-\frac23 & \frac13 & \frac13
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Soal 8

Carilah invers matriks berikut.

$$
B=
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 0\\
1 & 2 & 1\\
0 & 1 & 2
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Jawaban

Hitung adjoin:

$$
\operatorname{Adj}(B)=
\begin{bmatrix}
(2\cdot2-1\cdot1) & -(1\cdot2-0\cdot1) & (1\cdot1-0\cdot2)\\
-(1\cdot2-1\cdot0) & (2\cdot2-0\cdot0) & -(2\cdot1-0\cdot1)\\
(1\cdot1-2\cdot0) & -(2\cdot1-1\cdot0) & (2\cdot2-1\cdot1)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
(4-1) & -(2-0) & (1-0)\\
-(2-0) & (4-0) & -(2-0)\\
(1-0) & -(2-0) & (4-1)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & -2 & 1\\
-2 & 4 & -2\\
1 & -2 & 3
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Hitung determinan:

$$
\det(B)
=2(2\cdot2-1\cdot1)-1(1\cdot2-1\cdot0)+0(1\cdot1-2\cdot0)
$$

$$
=2(4-1)-1(2-0)+0
$$

$$
=6-2
$$

$$
=4
$$

Menentukan invers:

$$
B^{-1}
=
\frac14
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & -2 & 1\\
-2 & 4 & -2\\
1 & -2 & 3
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac34 & -\frac12 & \frac14\\
-\frac12 & 1 & -\frac12\\
\frac14 & -\frac12 & \frac34
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Soal 9

Diketahui matriks

$$
C=
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & 0 & 2\\
2 & 0 & -2\\
0 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Tentukan $$C^{-1}$$.

### Jawaban

Hitung adjoin:

$$
\operatorname{Adj}(C)=
\begin{bmatrix}
(0\cdot1-(-2)\cdot1) & -(0\cdot1-2\cdot1) & (0\cdot2-2\cdot0)\\
-(2\cdot1-(-2)\cdot0) & (3\cdot1-2\cdot0) & -(3\cdot(-2)-2\cdot2)\\
(2\cdot1-0\cdot0) & -(3\cdot1-0\cdot0) & (3\cdot0-0\cdot2)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
(0+2) & -(0-2) & (0-0)\\
-(2+0) & (3-0) & -(-6-4)\\
(2-0) & -(3-0) & (0-0)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 2 & 0\\
-2 & 3 & 10\\
2 & -3 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Hitung determinan:

$$
\det(C)
=3(0\cdot1-(-2)\cdot1)-0(2\cdot1-(-2)\cdot0)+2(2\cdot1-0\cdot0)
$$

$$
=3(0+2)-0(2+0)+2(2-0)
$$

$$
=3(2)-0(2)+2(2)
$$

$$
=6-0+4
$$

$$
=10
$$

Menentukan invers:

$$
C^{-1}
=
\frac{1}{10}
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 2 & 0\\
-2 & 3 & 10\\
2 & -3 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac15 & \frac15 & 0\\
-\frac15 & \frac3{10} & 1\\
\frac15 & -\frac3{10} & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$
