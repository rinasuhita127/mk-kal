Soal tentang determinan matriks, dekomposisi matriks, dan invers matriks 3×3#

A. Soal Determinan Matriks 3×3#

Soal 1#

Hitung determinan dari matriks berikut:

\[\begin{split} A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban#

Menghitung determinan menggunakan ekspansi baris 1

\[ \det(A)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}(-1)^{1+k}a_{1k}M_{1k} \]
\[ =(-1)^{1+1}a_{11}M_{11} +(-1)^{1+2}a_{12}M_{12} +(-1)^{1+3}a_{13}M_{13} \]
\[ =2(4\cdot1-5\cdot2)-1(0\cdot1-5\cdot1)+3(0\cdot2-4\cdot1) \]
\[ =2(-6)-(-5)+3(-4) \]
\[ =-12+5-12 \]
\[ =-19 \]
\[ \det(A)=-19 \]

Soal 2#

Tentukan nilai determinan matriks:

\[\begin{split} B = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban#

\[ \det(B) =(-1)^{1+1}a_{11}M_{11} +(-1)^{1+2}a_{12}M_{12} +(-1)^{1+3}a_{13}M_{13} \]
\[ =3(0\cdot1-4\cdot5)-2(1\cdot1-4\cdot2)+1(1\cdot5-0\cdot2) \]
\[ =3(-20)-2(-7)+1(5) \]
\[ =-60+14+5 \]
\[ =-41 \]
\[ \det(B)=-41 \]

Soal 3#

Jika

\[\begin{split} C= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3\\ 2 & 4 & 6\\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban#

\[ \det(C)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}(-1)^{1+k}a_{1k}M_{1k} \]
\[ =(-1)^{1+1}a_{11}M_{11} +(-1)^{1+2}a_{12}M_{12} +(-1)^{1+3}a_{13}M_{13} \]
\[ =1(4\cdot1-6\cdot1)-2(2\cdot1-6\cdot1)+3(2\cdot1-4\cdot1) \]
\[ =1(-2)-2(-4)+3(-2) \]
\[ =-2+8-6 \]
\[ =0 \]

Karena

\[ \det(C)=0, \]

maka matriks C merupakan matriks singular , sehingga tidak memiliki invers.

B. Soal Dekomposisi Matriks (LU Decomposition)#

Soal 4#

Lakukan dekomposisi LU terhadap matriks:

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 4 & 2\\ 1 & 5 & 2\\ 1 & 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

dengan bentuk

\[ A=LU \]

Jawaban#

Misalkan

\[\begin{split} L= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0\\ l_{21} & 1 & 0\\ l_{31} & l_{32} & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad U= \begin{bmatrix} u_{11} & u_{12} & u_{13}\\ 0 & u_{22} & u_{23}\\ 0 & 0 & u_{33} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 1

Dari baris pertama,

\[ u_{11}=2,\qquad u_{12}=4,\qquad u_{13}=2 \]

Langkah 2

Hitung elemen kolom pertama matriks (L)

\[ l_{21}=\frac{1}{2},\qquad l_{31}=\frac{1}{2} \]

Langkah 3

Hitung elemen baris kedua matriks (U)

\[ u_{22}=5-\frac12(4)=3 \]
\[ u_{23}=2-\frac12(2)=1 \]

Langkah 4

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks (L)

\[ l_{32}=\frac{2-\frac12(4)}{3}=0 \]

Langkah 5

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks (U)

\[ u_{33}=4-\frac12(2)-0(1)=3 \]

Sehingga diperoleh

\[\begin{split} L= \begin{bmatrix} 1&0&0\\ \frac12&1&0\\ \frac12&0&1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

dan

\[\begin{split} U= \begin{bmatrix} 2&4&2\\ 0&3&1\\ 0&0&3 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jadi,

\[ A=LU. \]

Soal 5#

Tentukan matriks (L) dan (U) dari

\[\begin{split} B= \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&1\\ 2&5&3\\ 4&10&8 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

menggunakan metode eliminasi Gauss.

Jawaban#

Matriks awal

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&1\\ 2&5&3\\ 4&10&8 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Eliminasi pertama

\[ R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1 \]
\[ R_3\leftarrow R_3-4R_1 \]

diperoleh

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&1\\ 0&1&1\\ 0&2&4 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Faktor eliminasi:

\[ l_{21}=2,\qquad l_{31}=4 \]

Eliminasi kedua

\[ R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_2 \]

diperoleh

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&1\\ 0&1&1\\ 0&0&2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Faktor eliminasi

\[ l_{32}=2 \]

Sehingga

\[\begin{split} U= \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&1\\ 0&1&1\\ 0&0&2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

dan

\[\begin{split} L= \begin{bmatrix} 1&0&0\\ 2&1&0\\ 4&2&1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jadi,

\[ B=LU. \]

Soal 6#

Dekomposisikan matriks berikut menjadi (LU)

\[\begin{split} C= \begin{bmatrix} 4&2&0\\ 2&5&1\\ 0&1&3 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban#

Misalkan

\[\begin{split} L= \begin{bmatrix} 1&0&0\\ l_{21}&1&0\\ l_{31}&l_{32}&1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad U= \begin{bmatrix} u_{11}&u_{12}&u_{13}\\ 0&u_{22}&u_{23}\\ 0&0&u_{33} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 1

Baris pertama

\[ u_{11}=4,\qquad u_{12}=2,\qquad u_{13}=0 \]

Langkah 2

Hitung elemen kolom pertama matriks (L)

\[ l_{21}=\frac24=\frac12,\qquad l_{31}=0 \]

Langkah 3

Hitung elemen baris kedua matriks (U)

\[ u_{22}=5-\frac12(2)=4 \]
\[ u_{23}=1-\frac12(0)=1 \]

Langkah 4

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks (L)

\[ l_{32}=\frac{1-0(2)}{4}=\frac14 \]

Langkah 5

Hitung elemen terakhir matriks (U)

\[ u_{33}=3-0(0)-\frac14(1)=\frac{11}{4} \]

Sehingga

\[\begin{split} L= \begin{bmatrix} 1&0&0\\ \frac12&1&0\\ 0&\frac14&1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

dan

\[\begin{split} U= \begin{bmatrix} 4&2&0\\ 0&4&1\\ 0&0&\frac{11}{4} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jadi,

\[ C=LU. \]

C. Soal Invers Matriks 3×3#

Soal 7#

Tentukan invers dari matriks berikut.

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 1\\ 2 & 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Menggunakan metode adjoin atau eliminasi Gauss-Jordan.

Jawaban#

Menghitung menggunakan adjoin.

Rumus matriks adjoin:

\[ (\operatorname{Adj}A)_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}M_{ji} \]

Sehingga,

\[\begin{split} \operatorname{Adj}(A)= \begin{bmatrix} (-1)^{1+1}M_{11} & (-1)^{2+1}M_{21} & (-1)^{3+1}M_{31}\\ (-1)^{1+2}M_{12} & (-1)^{2+2}M_{22} & (-1)^{3+2}M_{32}\\ (-1)^{1+3}M_{13} & (-1)^{2+3}M_{23} & (-1)^{3+3}M_{33} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} M_{11} & -M_{21} & M_{31}\\ -M_{12} & M_{22} & -M_{32}\\ M_{13} & -M_{23} & M_{33} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} \operatorname{Adj}(A)= \begin{bmatrix} (1\cdot4-1\cdot3) & -(2\cdot4-1\cdot3) & (2\cdot1-1\cdot1)\\ -(0\cdot4-1\cdot2) & (1\cdot4-1\cdot2) & -(1\cdot1-1\cdot0)\\ (0\cdot3-1\cdot2) & -(1\cdot3-2\cdot2) & (1\cdot1-2\cdot0) \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} (4-3) & -(8-3) & (2-1)\\ -(0-2) & (4-2) & -(1-0)\\ (0-2) & -(3-4) & (1-0) \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -5 & 1\\ 2 & 2 & -1\\ -2 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung determinan:

\[ \det(A) =1(1\cdot4-1\cdot3)-2(0\cdot4-1\cdot2)+1(0\cdot3-1\cdot2) \]
\[ =1(4-3)-2(0-2)+1(0-2) \]
\[ =1+4-2 \]
\[ =3 \]

Menentukan invers:

Rumus matriks invers:

\[ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \operatorname{Adj}(A) \]
\[\begin{split} A^{-1} = \frac13 \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -5 & 1\\ 2 & 2 & -1\\ -2 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac13 & -\frac53 & \frac13\\ \frac23 & \frac23 & -\frac13\\ -\frac23 & \frac13 & \frac13 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Soal 8#

Carilah invers matriks berikut.

\[\begin{split} B= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0\\ 1 & 2 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban#

Hitung adjoin:

\[\begin{split} \operatorname{Adj}(B)= \begin{bmatrix} (2\cdot2-1\cdot1) & -(1\cdot2-0\cdot1) & (1\cdot1-0\cdot2)\\ -(1\cdot2-1\cdot0) & (2\cdot2-0\cdot0) & -(2\cdot1-0\cdot1)\\ (1\cdot1-2\cdot0) & -(2\cdot1-1\cdot0) & (2\cdot2-1\cdot1) \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} (4-1) & -(2-0) & (1-0)\\ -(2-0) & (4-0) & -(2-0)\\ (1-0) & -(2-0) & (4-1) \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 1\\ -2 & 4 & -2\\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung determinan:

\[ \det(B) =2(2\cdot2-1\cdot1)-1(1\cdot2-1\cdot0)+0(1\cdot1-2\cdot0) \]
\[ =2(4-1)-1(2-0)+0 \]
\[ =6-2 \]
\[ =4 \]

Menentukan invers:

\[\begin{split} B^{-1} = \frac14 \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 1\\ -2 & 4 & -2\\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac34 & -\frac12 & \frac14\\ -\frac12 & 1 & -\frac12\\ \frac14 & -\frac12 & \frac34 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Soal 9#

Diketahui matriks

\[\begin{split} C= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 0 & 2\\ 2 & 0 & -2\\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Tentukan $\(C^{-1}\)$.

Jawaban#

Hitung adjoin:

\[\begin{split} \operatorname{Adj}(C)= \begin{bmatrix} (0\cdot1-(-2)\cdot1) & -(0\cdot1-2\cdot1) & (0\cdot2-2\cdot0)\\ -(2\cdot1-(-2)\cdot0) & (3\cdot1-2\cdot0) & -(3\cdot(-2)-2\cdot2)\\ (2\cdot1-0\cdot0) & -(3\cdot1-0\cdot0) & (3\cdot0-0\cdot2) \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} (0+2) & -(0-2) & (0-0)\\ -(2+0) & (3-0) & -(-6-4)\\ (2-0) & -(3-0) & (0-0) \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 2 & 0\\ -2 & 3 & 10\\ 2 & -3 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung determinan:

\[ \det(C) =3(0\cdot1-(-2)\cdot1)-0(2\cdot1-(-2)\cdot0)+2(2\cdot1-0\cdot0) \]
\[ =3(0+2)-0(2+0)+2(2-0) \]
\[ =3(2)-0(2)+2(2) \]
\[ =6-0+4 \]
\[ =10 \]

Menentukan invers:

\[\begin{split} C^{-1} = \frac{1}{10} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 2 & 0\\ -2 & 3 & 10\\ 2 & -3 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac15 & \frac15 & 0\\ -\frac15 & \frac3{10} & 1\\ \frac15 & -\frac3{10} & 0 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
